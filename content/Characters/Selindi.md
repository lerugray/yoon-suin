# Selindi

![[Selindi.png]]

Human woman, low caste. **Cell recorder** of the
[[Congregation of the Unwashed]]. Speaks four languages. Pragmatic
and competent — runs the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse|safe house]] in
[[Vothrog]]'s absence.

## What she does

- Holds the cell roster and keeps headcount after a meeting scatters.
- Triages the cell's intelligence — produced the **anonymous note**
  about [[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore]] from the drop point in
  [[Turn 04]].
- Knows the long memory of the Yellow City underground —
  identified Constable [[Uvaris]]'s morning tea habit at the
  [[Amber Moth Tea House|Amber Moth]] in [[Turn 05]].

## What she remembers

In [[Turn 09]] she answered Vothrog's question about Cheth-of-the-
Salt-Shore alongside [[Tuket]] — confirming the Disturbance happened
roughly twenty-two years ago, that the official record's "eleven
dead" was suspicious for three days of confrontation, and that
Cheth was absent from the official record entirely — *not* among the
listed dead but taken deliberately by men who knew who they were
looking for.

## Status

Cell ally. Currently at the safe house with [[Dov]], [[Pav]],
[[Hamech]], [[Pip]], and [[Tuket]], holding six pamphlets for
distribution while Vothrog is south at the [[Tidal Mark]] with
[[Krah-Moh]].
