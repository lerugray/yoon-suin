# Constable Uvaris

![[Uvaris.png]]

Watch constable, [[Spice-Factor District]]. Off-duty but thorough.

Takes morning tea at the [[Amber Moth Tea House]] on [[Vellum Lane]]
**every day without exception** — a habit [[Selindi]] knew about
([[Turn 05]]) and Vothrog exploited.

## The Vellum Lane encounter

In [[Turn 07]] Vothrog engaged Uvaris directly at the door of the
tea house — asking whether *a man who enforced the movement
ordinances against the poor slept well at night* — and held the
constable's attention long enough for [[Pav]] and [[Hamech]] to
leave by the back, unseen.

Uvaris did not see them go.

But he will remember **a slug-man in plain robes who addressed him
with that specific question.** [[Vothrog]]'s face is now logged
against him. Vellum Lane is no longer safe.

## Status

Active threat. Will recognize Vothrog on sight if they meet again.
