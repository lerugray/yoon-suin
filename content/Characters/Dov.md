# Dov

![[Dov.png]]

Ex-dockworker, brother of [[Pav]]. Burn-scarred forearms — the
mark of the dock furnaces where the brothers worked before joining
the cell.

Cell member of the [[Congregation of the Unwashed]]. Quiet relative
to his brother; both at the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse|safe house]]
when Vothrog returned with [[Krah-Moh]] in [[Turn 04]].

See [[Pav]] — the brothers are usually mentioned together in
the play record.
