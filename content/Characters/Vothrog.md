# Vothrog the Wise

![[Vothrog.png]]

A slug-man **Heretic-Scholar** of the Congregation of the Unwashed.
The player character.

> Vothrog preaches that the divine spark resides in every body
> regardless of species or caste — a protocommunist anabaptist tuned
> to a setting of caste-locked god-emperors and opium-flooded slums.
> He carries hand-copied pamphlets titled *"The Slime-Lords Drink What
> the Wretched Weep"* and signs none of them.
