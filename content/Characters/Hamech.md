# Hamech

![[Hamech.png]]

Human dockworker, low-merchant caste. **New recruit to the
[[Congregation of the Unwashed]]** — on only his second meeting when
the Watch raided the Copper Ghetto in [[Turn 01]].

## The weak-link problem

- Knew no safe-house protocols.
- Had seen [[Vothrog]]'s face.
- When the meeting scattered, fled to a place he habitually used
  when frightened: the [[Amber Moth Tea House]] on
  [[Vellum Lane]].

[[Selindi]] flagged the hazard immediately ([[Turn 04]]): the Amber
Moth was Constable [[Uvaris]]'s morning haunt. If Hamech was there
when Uvaris arrived for his tea, the cell was one conversation away
from compromise.

## Extraction

- **[[Turn 06]]** — Vothrog, [[Pav]], and [[Krah-Moh]] to Vellum
  Lane at first light, ahead of Uvaris.
- **[[Turn 07]]** — Vothrog engaged Uvaris at the door of the tea
  house while Pav slipped inside and brought Hamech out the back.
  Uvaris did not see them go.
- **[[Turn 08]]** — Vothrog corrected Hamech back at the safe
  house: *familiar haunts are exactly where not to go when things
  break apart.*

## Status

Currently at the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse]]. Corrected on protocol
but still the cell's most vulnerable member. Knows Vothrog's face
and species; if he is taken, the cell is exposed.
