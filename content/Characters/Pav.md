# Pav

![[Pav.png]]

Ex-dockworker, brother of [[Dov]]. Burn-scarred forearms from the
dock furnaces. Cell member of the [[Congregation of the Unwashed]].

## What he's done

- **[[Turn 06]]** — Accompanied Vothrog and Krah-Moh to the
  [[Amber Moth Tea House]] in the [[Spice-Factor District]] at first
  light.
- **[[Turn 07]]** — Extracted [[Hamech]] from the tea house's back
  rooms while Vothrog held Constable [[Uvaris]]'s attention at the
  door. Knew Hamech personally (recruited him, or recognized him
  from the dock days).

Pav is the cell member Vothrog reaches for when the job is small,
specific, and physical.
