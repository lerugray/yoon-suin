# The Ward of the Salt Shore

A ruined ward in [[Old Town]] south of the [[Yellow City]]. The
quarter where [[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore]] preached and where the
[[Salt Shore Disturbance]] was put down twenty-two years ago.

Living witnesses — including the unnamed
[[Old crab-man at the tidal mark|old crab-man]] — still tend the
[[Tidal Mark]].

## Vothrog's current location

As of [[Turn 15]], Vothrog stands at the [[Tidal Mark]] in this ward
with [[Krah-Moh]] at his shoulder and the old crab-man newly a
fragile ally.

## What's in the ward

- [[Tidal Mark]] — *the waterline where the offerings have been
  kept for twenty-two years.*
