# The Canal Quarter

A waterway quarter of the [[Yellow City]] — back routes among the
canals, doors that open onto piers, kitchens above moored barges.

Home to the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse]] where the cell regrouped
after the [[Copper Ghetto]] raid.
