# Vellum Lane

A street in the [[Spice-Factor District]] of the [[Yellow City]].
Site of the [[Amber Moth Tea House]].

## What happened here

- [[Turn 06]] — Vothrog approached the lane with [[Pav]] and
  [[Krah-Moh]] at first light, planning to extract [[Hamech]] before
  Constable [[Uvaris]] arrived for his morning tea.
- [[Turn 07]] — At the door of the Amber Moth, Vothrog engaged
  Uvaris directly with the question *"do you sleep well at
  night?"* while Pav slipped inside and brought Hamech out unseen.
  Uvaris did not see them go but logged Vothrog's face and the
  question.

## Status

**No longer safe for Vothrog or [[Hamech]].** Uvaris will recognize
a slug-man in plain robes on Vellum Lane on sight.
