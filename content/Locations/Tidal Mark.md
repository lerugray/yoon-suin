# The Tidal Mark

The waterline in the [[Ward of the Salt Shore]] in [[Old Town]],
south of the [[Yellow City]].

For **twenty-two years** the salt-flat workers have left offerings
here on **dark moon nights**: cracked shells arranged in
**congregation-circles**, cupped side up, with yellow flowers at
their centers. The line of offerings runs **forty yards along the
waterline** in various stages of age and weathering.

[[Tuket]] calls them *waiting offerings*, not *mourning offerings* —
a distinction that matters once you know
[[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore|Cheth]] was not killed but **taken**.

## Who tends them

The unnamed [[Old crab-man at the tidal mark|old crab-man]] — for
twenty-two years. He sat in the shadow of a collapsed archway twenty
feet back, watching, when Vothrog and [[Krah-Moh]] arrived in
[[Turn 10]].

## What happened here

- [[Turn 10]] — Vothrog and Krah arrive; the old man watches. Krah
  touches the mud at the mark with a flat claw — a gesture that
  read as reverence.
- [[Turn 12]] — Vothrog casts *Speak Truth to the Wretched*, spending
  1 HP, and speaks plainly through Krah's relay.
- [[Turn 13]] — Vothrog reads the **veth-cheth** wave-scar on Krah's
  shell.
- [[Turn 15]] — The old man recognizes the mark. Cheth is alive,
  somewhere distant. Krah came from that direction.
