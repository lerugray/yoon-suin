# The Amber Moth Tea House

A tea house on [[Vellum Lane]] in the [[Spice-Factor District]] of
the [[Yellow City]].

Constable [[Uvaris]]'s morning haunt — he takes his tea here **every
day without exception**, a habit [[Selindi]] knew about and Vothrog
exploited.

## Who was there in Turn 7

- [[Hamech]], who had fled to the tea house from instinct after the
  [[Copper Ghetto]] raid scattered the cell.
- [[Vothrog]] at the door, holding Uvaris's attention.
- [[Pav]] inside, extracting Hamech through the back.
- [[Krah-Moh]] in plain sight nearby, waiting to leave with Vothrog.

## Status

A place that almost cost the cell everything. No longer in scope as
a meeting or sheltering site.
