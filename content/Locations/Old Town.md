# Old Town

South of the [[Yellow City]] proper. **A ruined district** — the
quarter where the salt-flat workers once congregated under
[[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore|Cheth]], shattered by the
[[Salt Shore Disturbance]] twenty-two years ago.

Contains the [[Ward of the Salt Shore]] and the [[Tidal Mark]] where
the workers still leave offerings on dark moon nights.

## Status

[[Krah-Moh]] navigated the approach without hesitation in
[[Turn 10]] — using routes that suggested **he knew the ward
personally** before he ever met Vothrog.
