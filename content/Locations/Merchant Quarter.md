# The Merchant Quarter

A commercial quarter of the [[Yellow City]] — caravan houses,
counting halls, the private archives of the trading families.
Contains the [[Cartulary of Three Bells]].

Unexplored in play so far ([[Turn 09]] surfaced it as a lead via
[[Selindi]]).
