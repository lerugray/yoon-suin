# The Cartulary of Three Bells

A **private archive** in the [[Merchant Quarter]] of the
[[Yellow City]]. Surfaced by [[Selindi]] in [[Turn 09]] as the lead
on what the Oligarchy's public records had quietly **excised**.

> *Not sympathizers but archivists.* The Cartulary trades in things
> the official record does not contain. The [[Salt Shore Disturbance]]'s
> real headcount, [[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore|Cheth]]'s actual fate,
> and the names of the men who took him are the kinds of gaps the
> Cartulary might fill — for a price, or for a favor, or in exchange
> for something Vothrog has not yet realized he is carrying.
