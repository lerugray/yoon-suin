# The Canal Quarter Safehouse

The [[Congregation of the Unwashed]]'s current base of operations
after the [[Copper Ghetto]] raid. In the [[Canal Quarter]] of the
[[Yellow City]].

## Who's there

- [[Selindi]] — running the safe house.
- [[Dov]] and [[Pav]] — cell members.
- [[Hamech]] — extracted from the [[Amber Moth Tea House]] in
  [[Turn 07]], currently being held to safer protocols.
- [[Tuket]] — cell elder.
- [[Pip]] — ulufo cell member.

Six pamphlets are with Selindi for distribution; Vothrog took the
other six south with him to the [[Tidal Mark]].

## What happens here

- [[Turn 04]] — Vothrog returns from the Copper Ghetto with
  [[Krah-Moh]] and introduces him to the cell. Selindi reveals the
  anonymous note about [[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore]].
- [[Turn 05]] — Vothrog reads the note; surfaces Hamech's likely
  whereabouts (the [[Amber Moth Tea House]]).
- [[Turn 08]] — Vothrog returns with Hamech in tow after the Vellum
  Lane extraction; asks the cell if anyone has heard of Cheth.
- [[Turn 09]] — [[Tuket]] and [[Selindi]] answer; Vothrog and Krah
  prepare to depart south.
