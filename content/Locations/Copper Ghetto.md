# The Copper Ghetto

A low-caste quarter of the [[Yellow City]] — copper-stained slate
rooftops, dye vats, opium-smoke spirals. The
[[Congregation of the Unwashed]]'s old meeting place.

## What happened here

[[Turn 01]] — Vothrog held a congregation meeting in a **dyewright's
cellar** before dawn. Twelve attendees scattered when Watch lanterns
appeared in the alley below; a thirteenth, [[Krah-Moh]], was
cornered on the street and could not run.

Vothrog descended from the rooftop, secured his pamphlets in a
chimney gap, bribed the Watch with 4 gp ([[Turn 02]]) claiming Krah
was a Magistrate's servant whose collar had been left at home.

[[Turn 03]] — Returned to recover the rope and pamphlets, then
departed north with Krah toward the [[Canal Quarter]].

## Status

No longer safe as a meeting site. The cell has moved operations to
the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse]].
