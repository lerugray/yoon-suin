# The Yellow City

The metropolis of the Purple Land. A **caste society** — slug-men,
crab-men, dog-men, humans, ulufo, others; each caste has prescribed
quarters, languages, and work.

Vothrog operates underground, distributing handwritten pamphlets
titled *"The Slime-Lords Drink What the Wretched Weep"*. The
[[Congregation of the Unwashed]] meets in the [[Copper Ghetto]]
before dawn; the [[Canal Quarter Safehouse]] is the cell's refuge.
The [[Spice-Factor District]] is where Constable [[Uvaris]] enforces
the movement ordinances. The [[Merchant Quarter]] hides the
[[Cartulary of Three Bells]] and its excised archives.

South of the city walls lies [[Old Town]] — the ruins where
[[Cheth-of-the-Salt-Shore]] preached and where the
[[Salt Shore Disturbance]] was put down twenty-two years ago.
