# ❖ Turn 4

> “Krah-Moh is my friend and companion, a child of god and no threat whatsoever, please welcome him among us with open arms, one of 13 at the meeting tonight - assisted him in escaping guards after the meeting who were about to accost him. Whats the status of everything here?"

## Scene Two: The Canal Quarter — Dawn Breaking

![[Canal Quarter Safehouse — Turn 04.png]]

You plant your staff and let the words come without softening them.

*"His name is Krah-Moh. He came to the meeting of his own will, stayed when the others ran, and was nearly taken by the Watch for it. I told two constables he was a Magistrate's servant and paid four gp to make them believe it. He is a child of the divine spark the same as any of us — perhaps more so, since he has less reason to believe anyone means it."*

You look at Selindi directly. *"Welcome him. He cannot speak our tongue but he heard everything tonight, and he chose to stay."*

```
CHA task — persuade the cell to accept Krah-Moh  
Heretic-Scholar [Vocation] + Congregation of the Unwashed [Affiliation] both apply  
→ Positive double roll on CHA 13  
Roll: 2d20 → 3 and 11 — take the 11  
11 ≤ 13 — SUCCESS   Quality: 11   No pair.
```

Selindi holds your gaze for a moment. Then she does something you did not entirely expect: she bows her head, a small and genuine motion, and says something in Low Caste Argot that means roughly *welcome, stranger, you are not outside this circle.* She cannot know if Krah-Moh understands the gesture. She does it anyway.

Dov — the elder brother, the one with the deeper burn scars — nods once, wary but not hostile. Pav, younger, glances at his brother first before nodding. The cell is not warm. But it is open.

Krah-Moh watches all of this without moving. Then he does something deliberate: he lowers himself onto a coil of rope near the door, settles his claws across his knees, and becomes still. *Present. Waiting. One of us.* You aren't sure whether that is crab-man pragmatism or something more, and you find you don't need to resolve it tonight.

---

**The status report**, as Selindi gives it while Pav boils something that smells like grain mash and weak tea:

Six of the eleven who fled have made it to safe houses in the canal quarter — she knows this because runners came through in the last hour. **Three are unaccounted for.** Two of those she's not worried about: an old woman named **Tuket** who knows these streets better than the Watch, and a young ulufo who scatters like a roach when frightened and always turns up. The third is **Hamech**, a human dockworker of low-merchant caste who is new — this was only his second meeting, and he doesn't know the safe house protocol yet.

*"He may simply be lost,"* Selindi says. *"Or he may be in a Watch cell. He doesn't know enough to hurt us badly. But he knows your face."*

She pauses. *"He knows it's a slug-man. That narrows things."*

The brothers look at each other.

Dov speaks for the first time, voice low and direct: *"The pamphlets. How many did you place tonight?"*

*"Six,"* you say.

He exhales through his nose. *"Six is good. Six will move."* He doesn't say what he means by *move* but you know: hands to hands, docks to ghettos, the slow underground circulation that is the only press the wretched have.

Pav sets down three clay cups and one large shell — for Krah-Moh, without comment — and pours from the pot.

*"There is something else,"* Selindi says. She reaches into her robe and produces a folded square of paper, water-stained at one corner. *"Left at the drop point last night, before the meeting. I haven't shown the others yet."*

She holds it out to you.

```
CELL STATUS  
─────────────────────────────────────────  
Present:    Selindi (recorder, 4 languages)  
            Dov & Pav (ex-dockworkers)  
            Krah-Moh (crab-man, no Common)  
Accounted:  6 safe in canal quarter houses  
Missing:    Tuket (likely fine)  
            the ulufo (likely fine)  
            Hamech — new, 2nd meeting, no protocol  
                     knows your face; knows you're slug-man  
Pamphlets:  6 in circulation  ·  6 remaining  
Coin:       76 gp  ·  HP: 4/4  
─────────────────────────────────────────
```

The folded paper sits in Selindi's hand, damp-edged in the thin dawn light coming off the canal. You don't know yet who left it, or what it says.

**What do you do?**
