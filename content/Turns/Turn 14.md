# ❖ Turn 14

> quick question, what do you mean by the relay?

Krah-Moh is acting as your interpreter — a **relay** between you and the old crab-man.

The situation is:

- You speak Common and the High Tongue, but **not** crab-click language
- The old crab-man speaks crab-click language, but **not** Common
- Krah-Moh speaks crab-click language, and understands your intent well enough from context and gesture to translate across the gap

So the chain is: **you → Krah-Moh → old crab-man**, and back the other way.

When the old crab-man said something and Krah-Moh raised his open claw toward you — that's Krah-Moh holding the channel open, waiting for you to give him something to carry back across it. He received the old man's words; he's waiting to see what you want to say in response before he translates it.

The "relay is open" just means: the conversation is live, both parties are willing, and the next move is yours.
