# ❖ Turn 7

> I will instruct Pav to go in and extricate hammech as quickly as possible and return to the safe house - I will personally engage the constable and lecture him on his life choices leading him to be an enforcer against the poor, while remaining respectful - positioning myself to keep an eye on the door to see when Pav exits - signal for krah to wait where he is but watch for me and leave with me when I leave.

## Scene Three: Vellum Lane — The Long Walk

![[Vellum Lane — Turn 7.png]]

Four seconds. You do it all in four seconds.

You catch Krah-Moh's compound eyes across the lane and hold up one flat palm — *stay.* Then a slow circle of the same hand — *watch. Follow when I move.* A dip of the ochre-shelled head, that millimeter nod. He presses further into the warehouse shadow.

You lean close to Pav and say two words. *"Go. Now."*

He's already moving, pulling his collar up, walking through the open front panels of the Amber Moth with the loose-limbed certainty of a dockworker who has been in every tea house in this quarter at every hour. You watch him long enough to see him slide into the chair across from Hamech and lean forward — then you turn, and walk up Vellum Lane toward the approaching constable.

---

Uvaris is a broad man, as you noted, with a jaw built for grinding difficult mornings into something manageable. He wears his Watch gray like a second skin even off-duty. He has the lamp still on his belt. He is, by every signal he projects, a man who has done the same morning for fifteen years and fully intends to do it for fifteen more.

You intercept him eight feet from the Amber Moth's door.

*"Constable."*

You say it with the particular weight slug-men give a word when they wish it to function as punctuation — a full stop placed in someone else's sentence. You do not step into his path. You step *adjacent* to it, staff planted, so that to ignore you he would have to physically choose to walk past a slug-man who has addressed him by title. The yellow stones of Vellum Lane glow behind him.

```
CHA task — engage Uvaris in genuine theological-political discourse
Heretic-Scholar [Vocation] applies — this is the sermon, not a deception
Positive double roll on CHA 13
2d20 → 9 and 17 — take the 9
9 ≤ 13 — SUCCESS   Quality: 9
```

He stops. Looks at you. *"Aye?"* Not hostile. Curious, in the way a man is curious about a stone in his path — he'll move it in a moment, but he'll look at it first.

You do not lecture. That would be too easy for him to dismiss. Instead you ask him something, in the measured and genuinely interested tone of a scholar who has found an unexpected specimen:

*"Do you sleep well?"*

A beat.

*"Beg pardon?"*

*"I mean no disrespect. I have spent a considerable portion of this night in contemplation of the relationship between labor and conscience. I find myself wondering, concretely, whether a man who spends his evenings enforcing the movement ordinances against those who have nowhere particularly safe to go — whether such a man sleeps well, and if so, how he manages it. I am genuinely asking."*

Uvaris looks at you. He looks at your plain robes, your quarterstaff, your slug-man's face in the early gold light. He is trying to find the angle — the scam, the politics, the aristocratic condescension he knows how to handle. He cannot quite find it, because there isn't one. You are simply asking.

*"I sleep fine,"* he says, after a moment.

*"I believe you,"* you say. *"That interests me more than the alternative, actually. I suspect the Watch does not attract men who find it easy. I suspect it attracts men who have decided that order, as imperfect as it is, is better than the chaos of its absence. That is a legitimate position. I hold a different one. But I wonder sometimes whether the men who hold yours have had cause to examine it recently, or whether it has become — simply — habit."*

---

In your peripheral vision: the Amber Moth's open panels. Pav's jacket. Then Hamech's sloped shoulders, following Pav out, moving east on Vellum Lane, away from you, away from Uvaris — slow enough not to run, fast enough to be two men who have somewhere to be.

```

Gets Hamech moving but it takes ten seconds too long — Hamech is confused,
frozen, whispered argument at the table.
Does Uvaris notice the movement? 
d20 vs WIS 11 → 14 — 14 > 11 — he fails to look.
His attention is on you.
```

Uvaris is looking at you. Only at you. His morning is not going the way mornings usually go and he is, despite himself, *thinking.*

*"That's—"* He stops. Starts again. *"That's a strange thing to say to a man on his way to tea."*

*"Yes,"* you agree pleasantly. *"I apologize for the inconvenience. I find mornings clarifying. I hope yours is the same."*

You let silence do the work. Then you incline your head, the minimal bow that a slug-man gives a man he is treating as an equal, and you step aside.

He watches you for three full seconds. Then he walks into the Amber Moth.

---

You turn east on Vellum Lane without hurrying. Krah-Moh materializes from the warehouse shadow and falls into step at your left shoulder, half a pace back, as though he has been doing this for years. Ahead, you can see Pav's jacket, and next to it, Hamech's hunched form, moving toward the canal quarter.


You catch up to Pav and Hamech at the second junction. Hamech flinches when he sees you — a slug-man in plain robes appearing at dawn is not his normal experience — and Pav says something low and quick in Argot that makes Hamech go still and then go very slightly gray.

He looks at you the way people look at a thing they don't have a category for.

*"He wanted to know who you are,"* Pav says quietly. *"I told him you're our man. He'd like to know why a slug-man is our man."*

The canal quarter is ten minutes south. Hamech is here, whole, uncollared, uninterrogated. Uvaris is behind you ordering his tea and possibly thinking about the nature of habit. Krah-Moh walks at your shoulder like a shadow that has decided to keep you.
