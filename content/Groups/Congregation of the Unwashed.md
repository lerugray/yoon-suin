# The Congregation of the Unwashed

Vothrog's cell. A **heretical congregation** preaching that the
divine spark resides in every body regardless of species or caste —
a protocommunist anabaptist movement tuned to the
[[Yellow City]]'s caste-locked god-emperors and opium-flooded slums.

## What the cell does

- Holds dawn meetings in places the Watch is least likely to look —
  the [[Copper Ghetto]]'s dyewright cellars, the
  [[Canal Quarter Safehouse]].
- Distributes **hand-copied pamphlets** titled *"The Slime-Lords
  Drink What the Wretched Weep"* — unsigned, hand-passed.
- Proselytizes in the low-caste quarters where the official
  preaching has nothing to offer.
- Provides shelter for those the Watch has marked.

were subsequently confirmed safe.

## Open threats to the cell

- **[[Uvaris]]** has logged Vothrog's face. Vellum Lane is no longer
  safe for the founder.
- **[[Hamech]]** knows Vothrog's face and species. Corrected on
  protocol but still the cell's most vulnerable member.
- **The [[Anonymous letter writer]]** knows about the cell well
  enough to drop notes at the safe-house drop point. Friendly so
  far; identity unknown.
